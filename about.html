Company Overview

RDnx Industries Pvt. Ltd. is a transformer manufacturing company based in Agar Malwa, Madhya Pradesh. Established in 2024, the company is powered by a team with over 16 years of hands-on industry experience.

We design and manufacture safe, efficient, and dependable distribution transformers that meet BIS standards and customer-specific requirements. Our commitment to quality and innovation drives everything we do.
